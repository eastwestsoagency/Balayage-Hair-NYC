FAQ

Common Balayage Questions, Answered

What makes balayage different from traditional highlights?

Balayage is hand painted freehand onto the surface of the hair, which creates soft, dimensional color that grows out naturally. Traditional highlights use foils for a more uniform pattern, while balayage gives a sunkissed, lived in look with fewer visible root lines.

How long does a balayage appointment take?

Most balayage hair color appointments run between two and four hours depending on hair length, density, and whether a gloss or toner is added. Your stylist will give you a precise estimate during your consultation.

How often should I schedule a root touch up?

Most clients return for a root touch up every eight to twelve weeks. Balayage clients often stretch longer between visits since the dimensional color blends naturally as it grows out.

Can I combine a keratin treatment with balayage color?

Yes. Many clients pair a hair smoothing treatment with their color service. We typically recommend spacing the two appointments about a week apart so the color has time to fully set before smoothing.

Do you offer color correction for previous coloring mistakes?

Absolutely. Our colorists specialize in color correction, carefully assessing your hair's history and health before creating a plan that restores balanced, dimensional tone without compromising hair integrity.
